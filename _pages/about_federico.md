## Federico Banfi

##### Research Tech

I am a herpetologist specialised in the evolutionary history and functional significance of cephalic appendage in snakes and squamates. I have a deep interest in digitalization techniques and their use in natural history collections, which has been the main driver behind my training in 3D imaging techniques. I have experience with classical microCT scanning, diceCT, surface scanning, and 3D image segmentation of both soft and hard biological tissues using different imaging software.
